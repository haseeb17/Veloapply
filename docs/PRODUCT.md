# VeloApply

VeloApply is a review-first AI job application copilot and Chrome extension. It autofills application forms on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, and Taleo, tailors the resume and cover letter to each job, drafts screening-question answers, and waits for the user to review and submit. It is not a mass auto-submit bot.

## What VeloApply offers

- Chrome extension autofill across major ATS platforms
- AI resume tailoring and ATS-oriented formatting
- AI-generated cover letters and screening answers
- Job application tracking
- Multiple career profiles
- PDF resume generation
- Resume scoring

## Who it is for

- Experienced professionals applying on company career sites
- Career changers who need tailored materials per role
- Students and graduates who still want review-first applications
- Recruiters and career coaches supporting those workflows

The public GitHub list in this repository is aimed at experienced hires. Internship and new-grad hunting is already well served by other community lists.
